# Why Alphaline

Alphaline exists so that anyone can chart, draw and study price without a paywall in the way. This page sets out what prompted it, and keeps what is confirmed separate from what is reported. Last checked 24 November 2026.

## TradingView and drawings

**Reported, not confirmed by TradingView.** On 17 and 18 November 2026, users of TradingView's free Basic plan began reporting that they could no longer add drawings (trend lines, support and resistance levels and other annotations) after reaching about 30. Several said the count appeared to include saved drawings on other symbols, so drawings made years ago could block new ones.
- FinanceFeeds, "TradingView Free Plan Hit by 30-Drawing Limit, Users Say", published 18 Nov 2026, updated 19 Nov 2026: https://financefeeds.com/tradingview-free-users-report-new-30-drawing-limit/
- The article cites Reddit and community reports from 17 and 18 Nov 2026 and notes that TradingView had not published an announcement about the limit or explained how drawings are counted.

**Confirmed by TradingView:**
- Plan limits on things like indicators per chart, saved layouts and alerts are listed on TradingView's pricing page: https://www.tradingview.com/pricing/
- TradingView's help centre says a chart layout can grow too large to save, and suggests removing drawings when that happens: https://www.tradingview.com/support/solutions/43000484324-i-received-a-your-chart-is-too-large-and-cannot-be-saved-message/
- Separately, Pine Script indicators have long had a limit on the lines and labels a script can draw (about 50 by default, up to 500). That applies to scripts, not to drawings you make by hand: https://www.tradingview.com/blog/en/drawings-count-new-parameters-21535/

We found no TradingView pricing or help page, as of 24 November 2026, that states a number for manual drawings on the free plan. If TradingView publishes one, or the reports turn out to be wrong, we will update this page.

## What Alphaline does differently
- **No drawing limits.** Drawings are plain files in `data/drawings` on your computer.
- **No account and no subscription.** You bring the AI you already pay for, or none at all.
- **Your data stays yours.** Prices, drawings, briefs and notes never leave your machine unless you move them.
- **Open source.** Read every line, change anything, keep it forever.

TradingView is a capable platform and many traders will keep using it. Alphaline is for people who want their charts, and their AI, on their own terms.
