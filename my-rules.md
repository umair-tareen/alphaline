# My rules

Your AI reads this file before it marks a chart or writes your brief. Replace the examples with your own.
In the Alpha Trading Pros intensive, this is the file we build together.

## What I trade
- Tickers: SPY, QQQ, IWM
- Timeframes: 5 minute for entries, 1 hour and daily for context
- Session: regular hours only, 9:30 AM to 4:00 PM ET

## Levels I care about
- Prior day high, low and close
- Premarket high and low
- Opening gap: note its size, and whether it has filled
- Daily support and resistance zones with 2 or more touches

## Zones
- Mark an unfilled 15 minute fair value gap only if it is within 1 ATR of price
- Label each zone with why it matters, in 5 words or fewer

## When I sit out
- Within 15 minutes of a high-impact US release (CPI, jobs report, FOMC)
- When price is stuck between two levels less than 0.3 ATR apart
- After 2 losing trades in a day

## How to write my brief
- 10 lines or fewer, plain words
- Facts and my rules only. No predictions, no price targets I did not set.
