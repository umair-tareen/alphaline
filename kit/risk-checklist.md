# My pre-trade checklist

Your AI runs this before every trade idea. Any "no" means the trade is blocked, not just warned about.

1. Does the setup match an entry rule in my-rules.md, word for word? (yes / no)
2. Is there a scheduled high-impact US release in the next 15 minutes? (news_get) (must be no)
3. Have I already taken my max trades today? (journal) (must be no)
4. Have I hit my daily stop? (journal) (must be no)
5. Is the stop at a level on my chart, not a round number I picked? (yes / no)
6. Position size comes from risk_size, never from a feeling. (yes / no)

Change the list to match your rules. Keep it short enough to run in 30 seconds.
